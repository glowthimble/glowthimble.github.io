Jarloom Privacy Policy

Jarloom is a single-player game by Shoaib. The game saves scores, coins, cosmetics, progress and settings on your device without a game account. Android can back up this data according to your device settings.

Settings → Reset Progress deletes the local game save. You can also clear app data in Android settings. The developer does not keep a separate copy of your progress.

When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob may receive your IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, app interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers. Google uses these data for ads, measurement and fraud prevention and encrypts them in transit. Google manages ad data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Use Ad privacy choices in Settings when available to manage your consent. Android settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Contact Shoaib at contact.youplix@gmail.com with privacy questions.
